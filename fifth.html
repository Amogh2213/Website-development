💪 why your body needs exercise

Exercise is essential because it keeps your heart strong, builds muscle, and protects your overall physical and mental health

Physical activity strengthens your heart, builds strong muscles and bones, and helps control your body weight. It lowers the risk of serious illnesses

☀ quick health tips

  • 🍎 eat 5 fruits and vegies every day
  • 💧 drink 8 glasses of water daily
  • 😴 sleep 9 to 11 hours every night
  • 🏃 move your body about 60 minutes every day
